Hi, I'm Hannah Lovely Quinlog – Let Me Introduce Myself!

I was born and raised in Alicia, Bohol, in the beautiful Central Visayas region of the Philippines. Growing up in the province, I developed a deep love for nature and a strong appreciation for the warm, close-knit community that surrounds me every day. My childhood was filled with adventures exploring local beaches, helping with family farming activities, and learning the values of hard work and kindness from my parents and elders.

Currently, I am pursuing my passion for Computer Engineering and working to build skills that will help me make a positive impact in my community. I spend much of my free time with my family and making my projects. Beyond my professional interests, I also enjoy my hobbies in cooking, reading, and singing.

Looking toward the future, I dream of establishing an initiative that connects young people in rural areas with opportunities for digital skills training and career development. I believe that every individual has unique talents to share with the world, and my goal is to help create pathways for others to thrive. Whether through technology, education, or cultural preservation, I am committed to contributing to a more inclusive and prosperous global community where everyone’s voice can be heard.

Thank you for taking the time to get to know me – I look forward to connecting with you!
