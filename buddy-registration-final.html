Terms &
Conditions

Welcome to the Buddy Registration Program. By participating, you agree to be bound by the following terms.

1. Program Overview

The Buddy Registration Program is an initiative organized by the Student Affairs Office to connect students based on shared academic interests, hobbies, and goals.

2. Eligibility

  • You must be a currently enrolled student of the university.
  • You must possess a valid university identification number.
  • Participation is limited to one (1) active buddy registration per student at a time.

3. Data Privacy & Consent

By submitting this registration, you consent to the collection, storage, and processing of your personal data strictly for the purpose of buddy matching and program communication.

  • Your data will not be shared with third parties for commercial purposes.
  • Social media information is shared only with your matched buddy upon mutual confirmation.
  • Your profile photo will only be visible to your matched buddy and program coordinators.
  • You may request deletion of your data at any time through the Student Affairs Office.

4. Code of Conduct

  • Harassment, bullying, or any form of discriminatory conduct is prohibited.
  • Sharing a buddy's personal information without explicit consent is prohibited.
  • Providing false or misleading information in the registration form is prohibited.

5. Matching Process

Buddy matching is performed by program coordinators based on compatibility factors. Matches are not guaranteed.

6. Communication & Engagement

  • Inactivity for more than 30 consecutive days may result in deactivation of your registration.

7. Liability Disclaimer

The university assumes no liability for disputes arising from buddy relationships formed through this program.

8. Amendments

The university reserves the right to modify these Terms and Conditions at any time.
